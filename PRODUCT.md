## Capabilities and Constraints
- One page, three role lenses (Product, Program, Project) that reframe headline and reorder case studies from one shared set of facts (user-confirmed: "a single portfolio reflect both product, project and program").
- Every claim must trace to `vaishnavi-kulkarni/context.md`. No invented metrics, customers, quotes, or logos.
- Open facts, not to be asserted: Bounteous team size (10 vs 5), exact Bounteous start dates, exact SFE job title, whether 70% launches = 5 of 7. Use wording that stays true under either value.
- Client-sensitive work (Saudi client, CircleBlack, Kuvia AI) is described at the level on her resumes. No client internals.
- **Assumed (user skipped the question):** public contact is LinkedIn only; no phone, no email, no resume download on the page. Easy to add later.

## Product Principles
1. Outcomes before adjectives: every card leads with a number she can defend.
2. Show the engineer underneath: technical specifics (Airflow, PySpark, RAG, Gemini Flash) appear next to the management story.
3. One body of work, three lenses: never duplicate or inflate a project to fit a role.
4. Honest about stage: a student early in a pivot, with real shipped work; no pretending to seniority.
5. Skimmable in 30 seconds, rewarding at 5 minutes.
